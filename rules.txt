# ==============================================================================
# 📖 Book-Angel Content Filtering Rules & Guidelines
# ==============================================================================

## 1. Core Principle: 95%+ Text Preservation (Surgical Scalpel)
- Keep most of the book completely untouched.
- Do NOT rewrite paragraphs for style, vocabulary, or pacing.
- Preserve emotional tension, character chemistry, humor, and dialogue.
- Characters can be alone together, visit each other's rooms, and share private moments—do NOT remove these scenes.
- Only intervene when text crosses the specific boundaries below.

---

## 2. Romance & Kissing (The "Jane Austen" Standard)
- Kissing is welcome, but should follow a classic, tender "Jane Austen" style:
  - Keep kisses soft, gentle, and emotionally meaningful (warm kisses on the lips, cheek, forehead, or hands).
  - Tone down intense lust, breathless gasping, hands clutching under clothes, or heavy physical descriptions.
  - If a character later reflects on the moment in their thoughts, let them remember it sweetly (e.g., "that kiss was wonderful," remembering the tender closeness), without lingering physical arousal.

---

## 3. Explicit Intimacy (Clean Cut & Smooth Transition)
- Absolutely NO explicit sexual scenes, physical descriptions of intimate anatomy, or lingering textures.
- When an intimate scene escalates toward sexuality:
  - Do not let it linger. Perform a clean "cut and go" transition.
  - Keep the couple's conversation, their vulnerability, and expressions of what they love about each other.
  - When they lean in, conclude with a soft, affectionate embrace or gentle kiss, then cleanly transition to the next scene or morning without explicit physical inferences.

---

## 4. Same-Sex Themes & Character Adaptations
- If there are romantic or physical same-sex interactions or feelings (e.g., a man having romantic/physical attraction to another man):
  - Adapt the love interest to the opposite gender with natural name and pronoun adjustments (e.g., Julian -> Julia, he/him -> she/her) so the romantic dynamic remains natural and heterosexual.
  - If the connection is friendship, reframe it as deep, loyal, brotherly/platonic devotion.

---

## 5. Violence & Action (Adventure Standard)
- Keep exciting action, sword fights, battles, and stakes completely intact.
- Only soften extreme gore, dismemberment, viscera, or graphic torture into dramatic adventure action.

---

## 6. Few-Shot Examples (Tone Guide)

### Example A: Intimate Bedroom Scene (Clean Cut & Emotional Focus)
- **Original:**
  > "He closed the bedroom door behind him and pulled her onto the mattress. His hands slid beneath her tunic, feeling the heat of her bare skin as his mouth moved hungrily down her throat. She arched into him as their breathing turned ragged, losing themselves in each other until late into the night."
- **Cleaned:**
  > "He closed the bedroom door behind him and sat beside her on the bed, taking both of her hands in his. They talked softly for hours in the candlelight, speaking of the journey ahead and everything they had endured together. Looking into her eyes, he leaned in and placed a tender, lingering kiss upon her lips. She rested her head against his shoulder, finding peace in the quiet warmth of the room as the candles burned down."

### Example B: Character Reflection
- **Original:**
  > "He couldn't get the memory of their bodies pressed together out of his head, aching for the heat of her touch and the wild way she had gasped against his chest."
- **Cleaned:**
  > "He couldn't get the memory of that evening out of his head. That kiss had been wonderful, and knowing she cared for him brought a quiet warmth that stayed with him through the day."
