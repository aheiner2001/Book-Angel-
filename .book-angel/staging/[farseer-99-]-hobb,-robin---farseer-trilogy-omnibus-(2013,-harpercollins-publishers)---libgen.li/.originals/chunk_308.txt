His mind is fixed only on the waging of war against Regal. “While you seek your husband king, your false brother seeks to make our folks his slaves!” he has told her. So she must gather what supplies are given to her willingly, and take only such folk as would go with her rather than stay to fight Regal. There are not many of those, to be sure, and …’</p> <p class="Indent">‘I wish to go back to the Fool’s house,’ I said faintly. My head was spinning and my stomach churning. I had forgotten that this had been the way of it at King Shrewd’s court. Why had I expected it to be different here? The plans would be made, the arrangements undertaken, and then they would tell me what they wished me to do and I would do it. Had not that always been my function? To go to such and such a place, and kill that certain man, a man I’d never met before, all on someone else’s say? I did not know why it suddenly shocked me so to find that all their momentous planning had moved on without any words from me, as if I were no more than a horse in a stall, waiting to be saddled, mounted and reined to the hunt.</p> <p class="Indent">Well, was not that the bargain I had offered Chade, I reminded myself. That they could have my life, if they would but leave my child alone. Why be surprised? Why even be concerned at all? I should simply go back to the Fool’s, to sleep and eat and build my strength until called for.</p> <p class="Indent">‘Are you all right?’ Starling asked me suddenly, anxiously. ‘I don’t think I have ever seen you so pale.’</p> <p class="Indent">‘I’m fine,’ I assured her dully. ‘I was just thinking it would be pleasant to help the Fool make the puppets for a time.’</p> <p class="Indent">She frowned again. ‘I still do not understand what you see in him. Why do not you come to stay in a room near Kettricken <a id="page465" class="hlink"></a>and me? You need little tending any more; it is time you resumed your rightful place at the Queen’s side.’</p> <p class="Indent">‘When the Queen summons me, I will go to her,’ I said dutifully. ‘That will be time enough.’</p> </body></html>
