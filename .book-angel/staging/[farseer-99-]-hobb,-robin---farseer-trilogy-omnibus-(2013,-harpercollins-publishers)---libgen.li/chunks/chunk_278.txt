I had not had fresh meat in some days, and the hot crackling fat on the pork almost made me dizzy with its savour. The bread was fragrant, the butter sweet. I had not tasted food this good since Buckkeep. For a second my appetite was all I considered. Then the taste of the apple wine put me suddenly in mind of Rurisk and how he had died of poisoned wine. I didn’t answer her question. I only said, ‘It’s a foolish time to even think of going to the Mountains. No one’s going to the Mountains.’</p> <p class="Indent">She nodded her agreement. ‘I understand that the King’s Guards pressed two barges and their crews a week ago, and forced them to attempt the trip.
