I barely resisted Verity’s impulse in me to fling himself on his knees at the feet of this woman and declare her far too royal for his insignificant love. My guts turned to ice when Brawndy told me, ‘You may bid my daughter farewell, if you wish.’</p> <p class="Indent">There were few things I wished less to do. So I bowed and excused myself, and forced myself to cross the rain-pelted garden, to present myself to Celerity. <a id="page491" class="hlink"></a>Faith and Shells immediately withdrew to a not-quite-discreet distance to watch us.</p> <p class="Indent">I bowed to her with absolute correctness. ‘Lady Celerity, I must thank you again for the scroll you sent me,’ I said awkwardly.
